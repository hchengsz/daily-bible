# Westminster Confession of Faith

Public-domain English confession (1647), Westminster Assembly. The bundled JSON contains all 33 chapters and 172 sections, including source metadata and Scripture proof references. The reader displays the `Content` text without the proof apparatus.

Transcription: https://github.com/NonlinearFruit/Creeds.json/blob/master/creeds/westminster_confession_of_faith.json

Source edition: https://archive.org/details/humbleadviceofas00west/mode/2up

Retrieved 2026-10-03. The transcription identifies its source attribution as Public Domain. No modern Chinese translation is included.

Daily plan: one section per calendar day, repeating after 172 sections, restarting at January 1. February 29 repeats February 28, matching the Catholic plan's leap-day convention. Catholic and Protestant completion records use separate task keys.
